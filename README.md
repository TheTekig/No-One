<h2>🕯️ About</h2>

<p>
  <strong>NO ONE</strong> is a short narrative game inspired by the visual and technical limitations of the original PlayStation.
</p>

<p>
  Set during a fictional world war, the player takes the role of a young medic assigned to a military battalion. Before entering the battlefield, he meets the soldiers who will become his friends.
</p>

<p>
  They talk. They laugh. They play games. They talk about home.
</p>

<p>
  Then they go to war.
</p>

<p>
  The player isn't there to become a hero.
  <br>
  <strong>He's there to keep people alive.</strong>
</p>

<br>

<blockquote>
  <p><i>"Do you know the price of a life?"</i></p>
</blockquote>

<h2>🪖 A War Without Heroes</h2>

<p>
The world of <strong>NO ONE</strong> is fictional.
</p>

<p>
Countries, factions and political conflicts are created specifically for the game. The player will learn about the war through conversations, documents, military orders and environmental storytelling.
</p>

<p>
The game intentionally avoids giving the player a simple explanation for why the war exists.
</p>

<p>
Because sometimes the most important question isn't:
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>“Who started the war?”</strong>
</p>

<p>
but:
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>“Was any of it worth the price?”</strong>
</p>

<h2>🎨 PSX Visual Direction</h2>

<p>
NO ONE isn't meant to look like a modern game with a CRT filter.
</p>

<p>
The visual style will be built around the limitations that made the original PlayStation unique.
</p>

<div align="center">

<table>
<tr>
<td align="center">◼️<br><sub>Low Poly</sub></td>
<td align="center">◼️<br><sub>Small Textures</sub></td>
<td align="center">◼️<br><sub>Low Resolution</sub></td>
<td align="center">◼️<br><sub>Vertex Jitter</sub></td>
<td align="center">◼️<br><sub>Dithering</sub></td>
<td align="center">◼️<br><sub>PSX Fog</sub></td>
</tr>
</table>

</div>

<p>
The project will also feature custom PSX-inspired shaders and rendering techniques developed specifically for the game.
</p>
